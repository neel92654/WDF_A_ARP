About Student Hub Portal

Student Hub Portal is a simple web-based platform designed for CHARUSAT students to access their important academic information in one place.

Purpose

The main purpose of Student Hub is to provide students with an easy way to access their attendance, assignments, courses, timetable, announcements, events and profile information.

Features

  • Student Dashboard
  • Attendance Details
  • Assignment Information
  • Course Details
  • Weekly Timetable
  • Announcements and Notices
  • Upcoming Events
  • Student Profile
  • Account Settings
  • Contact Information

Technologies Used

The current version of the Student Hub Portal is developed using HTML and CSS.
