About the Student Hub Portal

The Student Hub Portal is an academic management platform designed to unify university communications, academic monitoring, and campus event coordination for engineering students.

Mission and Objectives

Academic Transparency

Provide students with real-time insight into attendance records, subject schedules, and course curricula.

Streamlined Campus Engagement

Enable event browsing, registration, and announcements without physical notice-board reliance.
